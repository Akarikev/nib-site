Your cozy studio stays on your device.

Nib (Google Play Package ID: com.nib.game) is an offline-first cozy coding studio game developed by Elorm. We believe games should be a quiet, private space—so Nib is engineered to collect zero personal data.

✓ Zero Personal Data Collected ✓ Zero Ads or Tracking SDKs ✓ 100% Local Device Storage

1. 100% Offline & Local-Only Storage

Nib runs completely offline on your device. All game progress—including your coins, completed coding projects, equipment upgrades, room decorations, studio atmosphere, energy charges, milestones, and adopted desk cat—is stored exclusively on your own device using local application storage (AsyncStorage on Android/iOS and localStorage on web). We do not operate external game servers, user accounts, or cloud databases.

2. Zero Personal Data Collection or Sharing

We do not collect, transmit, store, sell, or share any personal information, personally identifiable information (PII), device identifiers, IP addresses, location data, usage analytics, or crash telemetry. Because Nib requires no account registration or sign-in, we never ask for your name, email address, phone number, or payment details.

3. No Advertising, Analytics, or In-App Purchases

  • No Ads: Nib contains zero third-party advertising networks or ad SDKs.
  • No Trackers: We do not embed third-party analytics or behavioral tracking scripts inside the mobile application.
  • No Real-Money Transactions: Nib contains no in-app purchases, subscriptions, or paid currencies. Every item in the studio is earned through in-game timed coding projects.

4. Device Permissions

Nib does not request or access sensitive device permissions such as your camera, microphone, contacts, photo library, or location. Our Android configuration explicitly blocks android.permission.CAMERA and android.permission.RECORD_AUDIO.

5. Data Retention & How to Delete Your Data

Because all game data is stored locally on your device and never leaves it, you have full control over your data at all times. You can permanently delete all saved game data at any time by clearing the app’s storage in your Android device settings or by uninstalling Nib.

6. Children’s Privacy

Nib is a wholesome, general-audience simulation game that does not collect personal information from any user of any age, including children under 13, in compliance with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), GDPR, and Google Play Families policies.
